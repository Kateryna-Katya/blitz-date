How BlitzDate Works

1

New Topic Daily

At midnight UTC, a new game-related topic is posted to the homepage.

2

Add Your Take

Type a quick response, whether it’s a deep dive, joke, or bold opinion.

3

React & Vote

Read others' takes. Upvote what resonates. The best rise to the top.

4

Earn Rewards

Get badges, keep your streak alive, and climb the contributor leaderboard.
